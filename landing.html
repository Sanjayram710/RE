Frequently Asked Questions

Which regime should I pick for FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26)?

expand_more
Under the revised Budget 2024 rules, the New Tax Regime offers a standard deduction of ₹75,000 and zero tax for taxable income up to ₹7.75 Lakhs (with 87A rebate). For most taxpayers with total deductions under ₹3.75 Lakhs, the New Regime results in lower tax liability. Our live simulator above tests your exact numbers.

Can I switch between Old and New Tax Regimes every year?

expand_more
Yes! Salaried taxpayers can switch between the Old and New regimes every assessment year at the time of filing their return under Section 139(1). Taxpayers with business or professional income (F&O, freelancing under 44ADA) can opt out only once in their lifetime.

How does SimpleGST protect me if I receive an IT or GST notice?

expand_more
Every return filed through SimpleGST comes with our Notice Protect™ guarantee. If a notice is issued, you simply forward it to our platform. A certified Chartered Accountant analyzes the notice, drafts the statutory response with legal citations, and uploads it on the government portal on your behalf at zero additional charge.
