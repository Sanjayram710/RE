4. GST Registration Eligibility & Status

Establish whether your business requires GST registration under the CGST Act or qualifies for turnover exemption.

Statutory GST Registration Thresholds (Section 22 & 24 CGST Act):

  • Suppliers of Goods: Mandatory if aggregate annual turnover exceeds &rupee;40 Lakhs (&rupee;20L in special category states).
  • Suppliers of Services: Mandatory if aggregate annual turnover exceeds &rupee;20 Lakhs (&rupee;10L in special category states).
  • Inter-State Outward Supplies: Mandatory irrespective of turnover under Section 24.
  • Voluntary: Recommended to claim Input Tax Credit and bill registered B2B clients.

Understanding the Inter-Head Utilization Rule (Section 49 of CGST Act):

Integrated Tax (IGST) credit must always be exhausted completely before utilizing CGST or SGST credits. Crucial: CGST credit can never be used to pay SGST liability, and SGST credit can never be used to pay CGST liability. Your remaining CGST/SGST liability of ₹77,436 is partially offset by your ₹15,000 electronic cash balance, leaving a final deficit of ₹62,436.00 to be paid through a GST PMT-06 challan.
