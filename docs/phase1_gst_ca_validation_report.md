### Executive Summary & Methodology

This statutory validation report critically reviews the GST Compliance & Data Architecture (CDA) against the statutory provisions of the **CGST Act, 2017**, **IGST Act, 2017**, **CGST Rules, 2017**, relevant CBIC Notifications, Circulars, and GSTN portal functional advisories up to November 2026.

Every finding is categorized under five standardized audit statuses:
1. **`VERIFIED`** — Legally and technically sound; validated against statutory provisions.
2. **`PARTIALLY CORRECT`** — Conceptually valid, but missing statutory parameters, thresholds, or recent amendments.
3. **`OUTDATED`** — Based on superseded legal provisions, pre-amendment rules, or retired portal procedures.
4. **`INCORRECT`** — Violates statutory law, judicial precedents, or GST portal filing validation mechanics.
5. **`REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL REVIEW`** — Subject to dispute, conflicting judicial interpretations, or taxpayer-specific litigation postures.

### Key Legal Conclusions & System Design Constraints

1. **Deterministic Rule 88A Optimization:** ITC setoff must be computed using linear optimization that guarantees zero statutory violations while minimizing net cash payable. No heuristics or AI guessing can be permitted.
2. **Reverse Charge Isolation:** Table 3.1(d) RCM liability must be separated strictly from outward taxable supplies and routed directly to cash challan generation.
3. **Statutory Filing Gateways:** GSTR-3B submission must be programmatically locked until GSTR-1 for the same period has been validated and furnished with an Application Reference Number (ARN).
4. **Transparent Simulation Mode:** In environments without live GSTN production credentials, mock responses, challans, and ARNs must be explicitly watermarked as `[SIMULATION MODE — NOT FILED ON GSTN]` with verifiable audit checksums to prevent misrepresentation.
